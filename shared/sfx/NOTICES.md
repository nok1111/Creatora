# Bundled sound effects

All sounds in `files/` are CC0 / public-domain — no attribution required.

- **DAEDAL Transition SFX Pack** (CC0 1.0) — whooshes, risers, impacts, clicks, glitches, projector loop. https://daedal.art/resources/transition-sfx-pack/
- **Clipabay Free Sound Effects** (CC0) — whooshes, UI, foley, ambience, comedy, stingers. https://clipabay.com/free-sound-effects

Per-sound source is recorded in `catalog.json` (`source` field). Files were
transcoded to OGG Vorbis q5 to keep the app bundle small.
